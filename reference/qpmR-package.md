# qpmR: Quarterly Projection Models for Monetary Policy Analysis

Build, solve, and simulate the semi-structural quarterly projection
models (QPM) used in central-bank Forecasting and Policy Analysis
Systems (FPAS). Start with
[`qpm_template()`](https://mustapha-wasseja.github.io/qpmR/reference/qpm_template.md)
for the canonical Berg-Karam-Laxton small open economy model, or declare
your own model with
[`qpm_model()`](https://mustapha-wasseja.github.io/qpmR/reference/qpm_model.md).
Solve with
[`qpm_solve()`](https://mustapha-wasseja.github.io/qpmR/reference/qpm_solve.md),
inspect dynamics with
[`irf()`](https://mustapha-wasseja.github.io/qpmR/reference/irf.md),
simulate with
[`stats::simulate()`](https://rdrr.io/r/stats/simulate.html), and
produce forecasts with fan bands via
[`qpm_forecast()`](https://mustapha-wasseja.github.io/qpmR/reference/qpm_forecast.md).
